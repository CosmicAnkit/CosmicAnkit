## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I'm an **M.Tech (Research)** student in Intelligent Systems at **IIT Mandi**, where I work on **multilingual foundation models**, **large language models**, and **speech technologies**.

My research focuses on the lifecycle of foundation models—from **large-scale data curation** and **continual pretraining** to **instruction tuning**, **preference alignment**, and **evaluation**. I enjoy building scalable systems that bridge research and engineering, with a particular interest in multilingual and low-resource language technologies.

I'm open for opportunities in Foundation Models, Multilingual NLP, and Language & Speech AI.

## 🚀 Selected Research & Projects

### 🧠 BharatGen Foundation Models
- Continual pretraining, post-training, SFT and preference alignment of dense and MoE multilingual foundation models.
- Built multilingual data curation pipelines processing **20,000+** books, research papers and web documents.


### 📖 ViBhasa
- Pretrained a **165M parameter Hindi language model** from scratch on **21B tokens**.
- Focused on efficient language modeling and long-context training.

### 🎼 Classical Music Benchmark
- Built a benchmark for evaluating lyric–notation consistency.
- Evaluated multilingual language models and analyzed their structural reasoning capabilities.
